How verification works

A clear, transparent process from application to live catalog.

1

Business application

Sellers submit company details, tax/ID documents, product categories, and contact information through Become a Seller.

2

Document review

Our team checks business legitimacy, ownership, and compliance with marketplace policies and local regulations.

3

Catalog quality check

Supplier listings are reviewed for accurate pricing, MOQs, images, and product descriptions before going live.

4

Verified badge issued

Approved suppliers receive the verified badge and can list products, respond to RFQs, and fulfill wholesale orders.

What we check

Criteria designed for B2B wholesale reliability.

Registered business

Valid company registration, operating address, and authorized representative contact details.

Legal & tax docs

Tax identification and any required licenses for the product categories being sold.

Supply capability

Evidence of inventory or manufacturing capacity that matches stated MOQs and lead times.

Quality & service

Clear return policies, responsive communication, and consistent product quality standards.

Account security

Verified email, secure login, and adherence to Pilot Sales Distribution platform rules.

Ongoing monitoring

Order fulfillment rates, buyer feedback, and periodic re-checks keep the badge meaningful.
