Release rule

Creative output cannot serve as inventory proof, and promotional source material cannot establish accuracy, performance, or buyer economics.

Why there is no open cart

A real wholesale purchase needs current inventory, an approved counterparty, exact freight and payment terms, and final buyer review. A public cart would imply certainty that this site cannot responsibly provide.
