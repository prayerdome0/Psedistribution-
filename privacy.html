Public-site data boundaries

The request builder operates in your browser and does not submit its fields to a server. Copying or downloading a draft is user-initiated and local.

What not to enter

Do not enter passwords, access tokens, payment-card data, bank information, private keys, or protected supplier and customer records.

External links

Opening a phone or WhatsApp link leaves this site and is governed by the destination service. Review the recipient and content before sending.

Protected business records

Company deal, supplier, and buyer records are maintained separately from this public site and are subject to their own access controls.
