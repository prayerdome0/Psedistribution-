Public-site use

This site describes an opportunity-review service. Published category descriptions are not offers, inventory commitments, price quotes, shipping promises, or purchase agreements.

The site does not create accounts, accept payments, place orders, reserve inventory, or authorize outreach. A request draft is not an order.
